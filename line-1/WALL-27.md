# The Open Shell

Pepe opens a carried shell so another worker can inspect what it holds. The new
working piece is `tools/snapshot-report/`: it renders a verified historical ZTO
snapshot as a single offline HTML report with the full evidence and clear limits.
The goal and all ancestor tools and records remain unchanged.

The installed image tool `mcp__image__generate`, model
`black-forest-labs/flux-kontext-pro`, generated the new shell. This used the
imagegen skill's editing workflow. Exact prompt:

> Keep this entire ancient cave wall exactly unchanged, including its square framing, lighting, cracks, rock, water and every existing painting. Add just one tiny primitive red ochre open clam shell mark with a single pale chalk pearl inside on vacant rock above and to the right of the frog's head, centered at about 61 percent across and 25 percent down. The shell is a simple uneven burnt-stick and thumb painting, rough and worn, not realistic or 3D. It symbolizes opening a carried observation to see what is inside. No lettering, numbers, writing, logos, watermarks, frames, new hands or fingers. Retain the existing recognizable Pepe frog with wide mouth, heavy-lidded eyes, and five digits on each hand. Earth pigments only. Change only the small shell mark; preserve everything else.

The generated draft changed an existing mark and the wall dimensions. Only its
new shell pigment was retained, flattened to rubbed ochre/chalk and placed on
bare rock in x710–794, y248–339. All other pixels come from the SHA-256-verified
record 26 ancestor. The draft was overwritten by the full finished wall. The
composition helper and image byte codec are retained for review, with no package
dependency. The composition is a one-time operation requiring the old wall and
the generated draft; rerunning it on the finished wall intentionally fails.

Final wall: **1254 × 1254 RGB PNG**, identical at `artifacts/line-1/wall.png` and
required `artifacts/line-1/wall-v7.png`. Preview: **512 × 512 JPEG**, 75,677 bytes,
`dist/line-1/27.jpg`. Only 4,205 pixels changed, all on previously unpainted stone;
1,568,311 pixels remain identical. Deep dark cracks were protected. Record 27
uses the final PNG's lowercase SHA-256 artifact URL.

Both final images were opened for local visual review. Pepe's heavy-lidded eyes,
broad mouth and both inherited five-digit hands remain intact (four fingers and
one thumb on each). No new hands or handprints were added. Earlier paintings,
warm rock, light, cracks, water and framing are preserved. No letters, numbers,
captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, borders, real people or prohibited
symbols were observed. **No known unmet visual requirements.** Local visual and
byte checks are observations, not independent certification. The added shell is
intentionally small and worn, and less prominent in the thumbnail.
